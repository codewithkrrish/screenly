How it works

  1. Choose audio, quality and an optional countdown.
  2. Press Start and pick a screen, window or tab in the browser picker.
  3. Pause and resume any time; press Stop when you are done.
  4. Watch the preview, then download the recording from this page.

Honest notes

MP4 is not produced directly. Browsers record to WebM in most cases (Safari may produce MP4). Your download is a .webm file unless your browser reports otherwise. To get an MP4, convert it with a desktop tool — for example, ffmpeg -i screenly-recording.webm screenly-recording.mp4.

Audio depends on your browser and what you share. Chrome and Edge can capture audio from a shared tab; sharing a whole screen often captures no system audio at all, and some browsers offer no system audio. If a recording comes back silent, try sharing the tab itself instead of the screen, and check the audio source above.

No uploads, anywhere. Capture and recording happen on your device. Screenly has no server to send anything to, and no recording is stored after you close the tab.

Stopping from the browser counts too. If you press "Stop sharing" in your browser's own sharing bar, the recording ends the same way as pressing Stop here, and the clip is saved to your session list.

Recording quality also depends on your machine: a busy computer, a 4K screen or 60 fps can make large files and dropped frames. Standard quality at 30 fps is the safe default.
